Zoetry Mallorca Wellness & Spa - Hyatt Inclusive Collection의 세계적인 서비스로 슈퍼스타 대접을 받아보세요

Located on a 14th‑century historic estate in the peaceful town of Llucmajor, Zoëtry Mallorca, part of Hyatt’s Inclusive Collection, is a luxury retreat surrounded by gardens, olive groves and Mediterranean nature. Just 10 minutes from the coast, this boutique resort blends elegance, serenity and authenticity in a truly unique setting.

Guests can enjoy an exceptional culinary experience across the resort’s four restaurants, including one awarded a Michelin star, where Mediterranean cuisine is crafted with local produce and ingredients from the hotel’s own organic garden. There is a bar and several outdoor spaces, that invite you to savour the true essence of Mallorca.

The rooms and suites, located across the Tramuntana and Ponent areas, combine contemporary design with Mallorcan charm and are equipped with everything needed for a comfortable stay. Some categories offer a private balcony or terrace with views of the gardens or the estate.

For those seeking wellness, Zoëtry Pure Spa features a heated pool, Turkish bath, steam room, ice fountain and a selection of personalised treatments using Natura Bissé® products. A space designed to disconnect and rejuvenate. (Adults only, 16+).

The resort also features two swimming pools, tennis courts, running trails, yoga experiences and guided visits to the organic garden. Its neo‑Gothic chapel, windmill and expansive gardens create a unique backdrop for weddings, celebrations and special events.

Just 15 minutes from Palma Airport and 20 minutes from the city centre, Zoëtry Mallorca is the perfect destination for those wishing to relax, enjoy nature and experience Mallorca with a touch of exclusivity.
